Ayyamul Bidh Fasting

Ayyamul Bidh fasting is a voluntary fast observed on the 13th, 14th, and 15th of each Hijri month. These days are called the “White Days” because the moon shines brightly at its fullest during these nights.

Muslims are encouraged to fast at least three days each month, and the most recommended days are the Ayyamul Bidh.

Evidence

The Prophet ﷺ encouraged fasting three days every month.

“Fasting three days of every month is like fasting for a lifetime.”

(Reported by Bukhari)

He also said:

“If you fast three days in a month, then fast on the 13th, 14th, and 15th.”

(Reported by Tirmidhi and An-Nasa’i)

Virtue

Fasting on Ayyamul Bidh carries great reward, as it is equivalent to fasting the entire year due to the multiplication of good deeds.

Important Note

This fasting does not apply on the 13th of Dhul-Hijjah, as it is one of the Days of Tashreeq, during which fasting is prohibited.

References: Bukhari, Tirmidhi, and An-Nasa’i.
