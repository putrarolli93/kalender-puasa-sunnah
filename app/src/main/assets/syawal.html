Six Days of Shawwal Fasting

Fasting six days in the month of Shawwal is a recommended voluntary fast observed after Eid al-Fitr. It carries great reward in Islam.

Timing

This fasting begins after Eid al-Fitr, starting from the 2nd of Shawwal until the end of the month.

Fasting on the 1st of Shawwal is not allowed, as it is the day of Eid.

Evidence

The Prophet ﷺ said:

“Whoever fasts Ramadan and then follows it with six days of Shawwal, it is as if he fasted the entire year.”

(Reported by Muslim)

How to Perform

The six days of fasting can be done:

  • Consecutively after Eid
  • Or spread out throughout the month of Shawwal

Both methods are permissible as long as they are completed within the month.

Important Note

For those who still have missed obligatory fasts from Ramadan, it is recommended to make them up before performing the Shawwal fast.

Reference: Sahih Muslim.
