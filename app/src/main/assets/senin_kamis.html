Monday & Thursday Fasting

Fasting on Mondays and Thursdays is a highly recommended voluntary fast in Islam. It is observed weekly on these two days and carries many virtues.

The Prophet ﷺ used to fast on Mondays and Thursdays.

“The Messenger of Allah used to choose to fast on Mondays and Thursdays.”

(Reported by An-Nasa’i and Ibn Majah)

Virtue

Mondays and Thursdays are the days when deeds are presented to Allah. The Prophet ﷺ loved that his deeds be presented while he was fasting.

“Deeds are presented to Allah on Mondays and Thursdays, and I love that my deeds be presented while I am fasting.”

(Reported by Tirmidhi and An-Nasa’i)

Special Significance of Monday

Monday holds special significance because it is the day the Prophet ﷺ was born and the day revelation was first received.

“That is the day I was born and the day revelation came to me.”

(Reported by Muslim)

Important Note

In addition to fasting, maintaining good relationships is important, as deeds may be withheld if there is conflict between individuals.

References: An-Nasa’i, Ibn Majah, Tirmidhi, and Muslim.
