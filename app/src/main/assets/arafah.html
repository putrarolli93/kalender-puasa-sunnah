Arafah Fasting

Arafah fasting is a voluntary fast observed on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah, one day before Eid al-Adha. It is highly recommended for Muslims who are not performing Hajj.

The Prophet ﷺ said:

صِيَامُ يَوْمِ عَرَفَةَ أَحْتَسِبُ عَلَى اللَّهِ أَنْ يُكَفِّرَ السَّنَةَ الَّتِى قَبْلَهُ وَالسَّنَةَ الَّتِى بَعْدَهُ

“Fasting on the day of Arafah expiates the sins of the previous year and the coming year.” (Sahih Muslim 1162)

Ruling

Scholars agree that Arafah fasting is recommended for those not performing Hajj. Pilgrims standing at Arafah are not encouraged to fast so they can maintain strength for worship.

Important Note

The exact date of Dhul-Hijjah may vary depending on the Hijri calendar used in different countries. Users are encouraged to follow local religious authority announcements for official observances.

References: Sahih Muslim (1162), Sahih Bukhari & Muslim, An-Nawawi’s Commentary.
