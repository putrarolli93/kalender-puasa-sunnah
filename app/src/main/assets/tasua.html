Asyura & Tasu’a Fasting

Asyura fasting is observed on the 10th of Muharram, while Tasu’a is on the 9th of Muharram. Both are highly recommended voluntary fasts in Islam.

The Prophet ﷺ said:

“Fasting on the day of Asyura expiates the sins of the previous year.”

(Sahih Muslim 1162)

Recommendation

It is recommended to fast on both the 9th (Tasu’a) and 10th (Asyura) of Muharram. This is based on the intention of Nabi Muhammad salallahu alaihi wasalam to differ from the Jews, who only fasted on the 10th.

“If I live until next year, I will surely fast on the ninth.”

(Sahih Muslim 1134)

Virtue

Among the days in Muharram, the most emphasized fast is on the day of Asyura. Fasting on this day brings great reward and expiation of sins from the previous year.

Important Note

The date of Muharram may differ depending on local moon sighting. It is recommended to follow official announcements from local religious authorities.

References: Sahih Muslim (1134, 1162), scholarly explanations on Muharram fasting.
